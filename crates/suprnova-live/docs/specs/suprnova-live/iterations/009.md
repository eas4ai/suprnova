# Suprnova Live -- Iteration 009

Status: Scope contract
Agreed: 2026-09-14

## In

This contract mirrors the Cairn commitment
`component-library-feedback-and-navigation`, agreed 2026-09-14 through
escalation `fdb-001`. Iteration 009 builds the feedback family and the
navigation family of the official component library under the Cairn
records at `docs/spec/component-library-feedback.md` and
`docs/spec/component-library-navigation.md`, which refine specs 24 and 22
into requirements with falsifiers and mechanisms. Its scope is the agreed
set FDB-001 to FDB-004, FDB-006, NAV-001 to NAV-004 and NAV-006:

- Alert, skeleton, spinner, progress, empty state, toast region and flash
  region; header bar, footer, sidebar with collapsible groups, breadcrumbs,
  tabs, pagination and load more, as Askama macros, one directory per
  component with a JSON manifest, installed by `live:add` and served
  beside the form and overlay families.
- Feedback presents state the server or the runtime holds: the alert's
  role follows its variant with a non-color cue per variant; a spinner or
  skeleton is bound through `live:loading` and shows on the runtime's own
  delay and minimum; progress is the native element with a value only for
  determinate work, a label and a readout; the empty state's reason is
  server state and its next action is the caller's; a toast announces once
  from a polite region without taking focus, and a critical error also
  renders in a persistent alert.
- Navigation keeps route semantics: anchors with real URLs, buttons for
  actions, `aria-current` from the server, collapse and local tab state
  held in the browser under stable keys, an explicit local or route mode
  for tabs, canonical page URLs in route pagination and a reflected query
  with `history.replaceState` and no history entry in Live pagination, and
  load more as a keyed append from a button that leaves on the last page.
- Tabs, listed under the overlays roadmap item, was never in OVL-001 to
  OVL-006 and lands here under NAV-002.

## Out

The notification bell and live feed (FDB-005) and the account menu
(NAV-005) stay with `component-library-live-native`; the stepper and the
command palette stay out by the 2026-09-13 ruling; the data-display family
stays with the commitment that follows.

## Implementation checkpoints

1. The fourteen component directories and the dogfood feedback and
   navigation galleries.
2. The mechanisms: the feedback and navigation tools, the dogfood document
   tests, the load-more morph fixtures and the browser cases.
3. The manual section, the changelog entry and the six mirrors.

## Definition of done

Every mechanism the commitment names passes on a committed tree and the
review at `.cairn/reviews/component-library-feedback-and-navigation.md` is
recorded.
